REFLECTIVE JOURNAL / 02

Learning Through Teamwork.

A reflection on collaboration using Tuckman’s five stages of group development.

REFLECTION GUIDE

Be specific.

Describe what actually happened in your group, including challenges, decisions and lessons. Do not invent disagreements or achievements.

GROUP WORK REFLECTION — COMPLETE WITH REAL EXPERIENCES

From individuals to a team

01

Forming

[How was your group formed? How did members introduce themselves, understand the assignment and divide initial responsibilities?]

02

Storming

[Describe a genuine challenge or difference in working styles. How did you communicate or resolve it? If there was no conflict, explain the uncertainty or negotiation you experienced.]

03

Norming

[What routines, roles, expectations or communication channels helped your team work together more effectively?]

04

Performing

[What evidence showed that the group was functioning well? Describe a meaningful contribution, result, or change in collaboration.]

05

Adjourning

[How did the project conclude? What feedback, appreciation, unfinished challenges or closure remained?]

My key lesson

[Reflect critically on what the experience taught you about communication, accountability, listening and future teamwork.]
