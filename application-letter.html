Application Letter.

A formal letter template for an industrial attachment opportunity.

APPLICATION LETTERINDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT

Kimberly Thato Lekgetho
[Professional email address]
[Date]

The Human Resources Manager
[Organisation name]
[Organisation city]

Dear Hiring Manager,

RE: Application for Industrial Attachment — Business Intelligence and Data Analytics

I am writing to express my interest in an industrial attachment opportunity at [Organisation name]. I am currently a Year One Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College, and I am eager to gain practical exposure to data analysis, information management and business decision-making.

My previous training in Health Care Assistance and experience in healthcare and data capturing have helped me appreciate the importance of accuracy, responsibility, communication and teamwork. I hope to build on these strengths while applying the knowledge I am developing through my studies.

I am particularly interested in [state a specific area relevant to the organisation]. I would welcome an opportunity to contribute with a positive attitude, willingness to learn and attention to detail.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be grateful for an opportunity to discuss my interest further. My CV is available for your review.

Yours faithfully,
Kimberly Thato Lekgetho

Replace all bracketed items; tailor the letter to a real organisation before using it.
