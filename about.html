Who I am

My name is Kimberly Thato Lekgetho, and I am a Year One student at Botswana Accountancy College (BAC), studying Business Intelligence and Data Analytics. I am motivated by continuous learning, personal growth and the opportunity to build a meaningful professional future.

My academic background

I hold a Certificate in Health Care Assistance from Boitekanelo College. Through my experience at Sidilega Private Hospital, I gained exposure to the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) and Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU). I also gained experience in data capturing, which strengthened my appreciation for accuracy, organisation and responsible information handling.

I am now pursuing Business Intelligence and Data Analytics to understand how data, technology and informed decision-making connect. This is a new academic direction, and I am eager to develop both analytical and practical skills.

My interests and strengths

My interests include technology, data analysis, business, communication and personal development. I value teamwork, attention to detail, responsibility and problem-solving, and I am particularly interested in how raw data can become useful information for organisations.

My goals

I aim to complete my studies successfully, strengthen my digital and analytical capabilities, and gain practical experience in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics. This portfolio documents the steps I am taking towards those goals.
